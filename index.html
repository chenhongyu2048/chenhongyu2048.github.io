About me

Background

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a Research Affiliate at the Emerging Parallel Computing Center (EPCC), advised by Prof. Shixuan Sun.

My research focuses on systems for efficient large-model training and inference. I previously worked on graph neural network systems. I received my master's degree from the National University of Defense Technology in June 2024 and my bachelor's degree in Space Science and Technology from Nanjing University in June 2021.
